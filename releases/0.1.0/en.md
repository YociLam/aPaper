# aPaper 0.1.0

English · [简体中文](zh-Hans.md) · [繁體中文](zh-Hant.md) · [日本語](ja.md) · [한국어](ko.md)

aPaper is an **AI-native research workspace** that brings literature discovery, paper management, evidence-based reading, knowledge building, and manuscript writing into one connected environment. It helps you carry a study from the first paper you discover to the research you create from what you have learned.

Research rarely follows a straight line. A new paper raises a question; a passage changes your understanding; an earlier finding becomes relevant to a draft. aPaper keeps those steps connected, so you can move between papers, ideas, and manuscripts without losing the sources behind them.

## From literature to manuscript

- **Daily Research — find your next lead.** Follow journals and preprint sources in your field, and browse new papers in one place. Recommendations include a note on why a paper may matter to your work, helping you decide what deserves a closer look.

- **Library — give every paper a place.** Keep papers, metadata, and research projects together, with bibliographic details close at hand. Import a PDF, bring in a folder, or make a one-time copy of your Zotero library to continue with the papers you already have.

- **Insight — understand the structure of a study.** Explore a paper's research question, method, contributions, and limitations through structured insights. Use them to orient your reading, compare approaches, and decide where to look more closely in the original text.

- **Mentor — ask questions and follow the evidence.** Discuss the paper you are reading, clarify unfamiliar ideas, and examine the reasoning behind a result. Answers are connected to passages in the paper, keeping explanation and evidence within reach of each other.

- **Knowledge — let understanding outlast a single reading.** Build a collection of concepts, methods, findings, and research questions linked to their sources. Review AI-generated candidates before keeping them, then return to the evidence as your understanding develops.

- **Research Workbench — turn understanding into a manuscript.** Organize writing by project, work in the LaTeX editor, and compile and preview the PDF alongside your draft. A research assistant supports revision, while file and project export let you take your work forward.

## Read with the source in view

- **Annotate as you read.** Highlight, underline, and add text or page notes directly in the PDF reader, keeping your observations beside the passages that prompted them.
- **Work across languages.** Translate selected text or a full PDF with a configured translation service, and return to the original to check terminology and meaning.

## Built around your research

- **Your papers stay close.** Papers, notes, and research knowledge are managed in a local library on your computer. Your reading and writing remain connected to the material you collected.
- **Choose your model service.** Configure a compatible provider in Settings for AI-assisted reading and writing. Library management and ordinary reading work without an AI model; AI features send the relevant content to your chosen service, whose pricing and data policies apply.
- **Work in your language.** The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

## Get started

1. [Download aPaper](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/releases/download/v0.1.0-b1001/aPaper.dmg) and follow the [installation guide](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/INSTALL.md).
2. Import a paper or your existing library. Add a model service in Settings when you are ready to use AI features.
3. Start reading, follow the evidence, and keep the ideas you want to build on. Check important AI-generated conclusions against the original sources.

Available for **Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14.6 or later**. Windows support is coming soon.
