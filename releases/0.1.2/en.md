# aPaper 0.1.2

[English](en.md) · [简体中文](zh-Hans.md) · [繁體中文](zh-Hant.md) · [日本語](ja.md) · [한국어](ko.md)

aPaper 0.1.2 makes it easier to trace answers back to PDFs and continue working while a reply is running.

## Research and PDF evidence

- Paper Mentor can search and quote attached PDFs. Click a citation to open the right page with the passage highlighted. You can also ask Paper Mentor to correct a Library paper’s bibliographic details.
- Paper Mentor, Knowledge Steward, and Research Assistant can read more PDF layouts, including some CNKI downloads. For PDFs with embedded files, the page contents are read without sending the embedded files to the model.
- Quoted follow-ups in Paper Mentor can run alongside the main reply, with independent progress and Stop controls. Queued messages keep their own files, and drafts with rejected attachments wait until you remove those files.

## Reader and previews

- Open attachments and Knowledge sources in the app. Preview full PDF pages, zoom documents, and mark up or crop an image to save a separate copy. Open supplementary papers in the reader’s second pane.
- Parallel translation stays visible for all target languages. If the PDF is already in that language, a notice appears and the button stays in its normal style. Deleting an annotation asks for confirmation.
- Set a keyboard shortcut for Quick Research in the menu bar. Press Escape while recording or use the clear button to remove it.

## Models and accounts

- New installations start with an empty model setup. Updates keep configured providers and models, and reset unsupported reasoning levels to Automatic. First-time custom provider setup gives more specific feedback for key, model-list, and local configuration errors.
- Ergouzi restores cached models at launch and refreshes them in the background. Unlinking removes its providers and selects a configured alternative when available. Registration and password reset show request status and a 30-second resend countdown after a successful code request. Passwords are hidden again when switching account forms.

## Download and installation

On an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14.6 or later, [download the DMG](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/releases/download/v0.1.2-b1008/aPaper.dmg), open it, and drag aPaper into Applications. To update manually, quit aPaper and replace the existing copy in Applications. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/INSTALL.md) for details.
