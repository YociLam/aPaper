# aPaper 0.1.4

[English](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/releases/0.1.4/en.md) | [简体中文](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/releases/0.1.4/zh-Hans.md) | [繁體中文](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/releases/0.1.4/zh-Hant.md) | [日本語](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/releases/0.1.4/ja.md) | [한국어](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/releases/0.1.4/ko.md)

## New Features

- **Language-specific announcements.** Open the island prompt or bell to read a message. Unread announcements open on the next launch. The Chinese versions of About also include the QQ group invitation.

## Improvements

- **Adaptive reading layout.** Narrowing the window hides the library sidebar, switches the reader to one pane, then hides the inspector. Widening restores the selected layout in reverse order.
- **Dogzee model defaults.** Paper Mentor, Research Assistant and Knowledge Steward default to GPT 6.2 Sol with medium reasoning.

## Bug Fixes

- **Message drafts.** Scroll inside long drafts and keep the composer height after sending.
- **Workspace recovery.** Reopen the main window from the Dock or Quick Research. Optional assistant profile failures no longer block entry.
- **Reasoning compatibility.** Retry another supported reasoning level when the provider rejects the selected one.

## Download and installation

On an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14.6 or later, [download the DMG](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/releases/download/v0.1.4-b1010/aPaper.dmg), open it and drag aPaper into Applications. To update manually, quit aPaper and replace the installed copy. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/YociLam/aPaper/blob/main/INSTALL.md).
